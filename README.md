Needle is a small handmade embroidery brand that creates unique and personalized products, such as tote bags, pouches, and customized gifts.
The brand focuses on turning customers' ideas into meaningful handmade pieces that reflect their personalities and emotions. Each product is carefully designed based on the customer's preferences, making every order special and personal.
Needle aims to offer creative, affordable, and customized embroidery products while helping people express themselves through thoughtful handmade gifts.
